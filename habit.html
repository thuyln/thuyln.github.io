GradiHabit

Small steps. Big changes.

Key Features

🎯 The Power of Focus

Limit your tracking to a maximum of 5 habits at once to avoid overwhelm and achieve the highest possible success rate.

📚 Science-Based Library

Includes 18 pre-built habits. Each habit comes with a detailed explanation of its benefits, scientific background, and step-by-step guidance following the 2-minute rule.

🌱 "Shrink It" Fallback

If you miss 3 days, the app suggests "shrinking" the habit into a tiny version (e.g., a 1-minute breathing exercise) to keep your streak alive without the pressure.

📝 Daily Review & Journal

At the end of the day, review your habits, adjust tracking times, rate your performance 1-5 stars, and write a micro-journal entry about your feelings.

📊 Visual Insights

Track your progress through a comprehensive dashboard featuring completion rates, GitHub-style heatmaps, and a radar chart analyzing the quality of each habit.

🔒 Ultimate Privacy

100% offline. All data is stored strictly on your device. Supports Light/Dark mode, 60 languages, and secure encrypted data backup.
